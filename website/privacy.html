Privacy policy

Early-release notice: GlobalChat is an MVP. This page is a starting template, not a complete legal privacy notice. The operator must add their identity, contact details, retention/deletion rules, hosting locations, legal basis, and applicable jurisdictional disclosures before public launch.

Information the current MVP handles

Account display names, phone numbers provided during registration, generated GlobalChat IDs, password hashes, conversation membership, message text, voice-note audio, timestamps, and basic server/security logs may be processed to operate the service.

Security and limitations

Passwords are hashed before storage. Traffic should use HTTPS. Messages and voice notes are not end-to-end encrypted; the backend can access stored message content and voice data. Phone numbers are not verified because OTP is not enabled. Do not use this version for sensitive or confidential conversations.

Storage

Voice notes in this MVP are stored in the application database as encoded audio. Database providers may impose quotas and retention policies. The operator should establish a tested process for account deletion, message deletion, backups, and incident handling before inviting real users.
